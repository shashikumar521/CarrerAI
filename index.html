CareerAI (CarrerAI)

AI Career Guidance Platform for B.Tech & Engineering Students

CareerAI (also commonly searched as CarrerAI or Carrer AI) is an engineering career guidance and placement readiness intelligence platform created specifically for B.Tech and B.E. students.

Core Capabilities for Engineering Students

  • Academic Analysis: Diagnostic evaluation of B.Tech CGPA, 10th/12th percentages, active backlogs, and academic trends.
  • Company Eligibility Checker: Instant criteria screening against cutoffs for 30+ top recruiters including Tier-1 product firms, high-growth startups, and mass recruiters.
  • Skill-Gap Analysis: In-depth matching against target tech roles such as Software Development Engineer (SDE), Full-Stack, Cloud/DevOps, AI/ML, and Data Analytics.
  • Personalized Career Roadmap: Structured four-phase preparation roadmap covering coding foundations, core CS subjects (OS, DBMS, CN), and project building.
  • ATS Resume Readiness: Recruiter-compliant single-column resume builder and automated ATS scoring.
  • AI Career Counselor: Interactive mentorship for placement strategy, off-campus hiring, and backlog recovery.
